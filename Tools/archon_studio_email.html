Partnership proposal

A proposal for official collaboration

 

Hello Archon Studio team,

My name is Alexey'a, and I made TMG Stats for the StarCraft: The Miniatures Game community.

The site is already online. Players can submit match results, view rankings and profiles, check statistics, and run leagues. Matches can also be submitted directly from Tabletop Simulator.

TMG Stats is not meant to replace your Command Center. I made it as an extra tool for players who want to track their games and take part in ranked play.

I would like to ask whether Archon Studio might be interested in using TMG Stats as an official community or ranked-play tool.

I am happy to change the site to match your requirements and work with your team on how it should be managed.

If this sounds useful, I would be glad to talk about it. You can also forward this email to the person responsible for StarCraft TMG or community projects.

Thank you for your time.
